Executive Summary

Oficio AI answers business calls and texts, captures customer intent, creates customer/job records, asks the owner to approve bookings, and keeps the owner dashboard current from Supabase.

Current product behavior

Customer Promise

Ana answers quickly, speaks in the customer's language, collects job details, checks booking availability, and keeps the caller informed without exposing internal workflow.

Owner Promise

The owner receives clean summaries, pending booking approvals, leads, call logs, messages, and customer history in one dashboard.

Approval Rule

Ana creates a pending booking request first. The booking becomes confirmed only after the owner replies YES. A NO reply keeps it as a lead.

Business Analyst Flow

This view explains the customer, owner, and operations journey without implementation detail.

Business process

Admin configures the business

Operations sets business name, owner contact, public/routing phone numbers, SMS state, services, service area, hours, timezone, AI name, summary language, and summary method.

Customer calls or texts

Twilio receives the inbound phone interaction. Calls go to Ana through the voice route. Texts go to the SMS route or can be forwarded by an owner in SMS transition mode.

Ana screens and handles the interaction

Ana checks if the caller is known, personal, vendor, spam, or a customer. Personal/vendor calls can transfer to the owner; business calls continue through intake.

Ana collects service details

Ana asks for name, phone if needed, service type, confirmed address, preferred time, and source. She checks business hours, closed dates, existing bookings, and service radius.

Ana writes records to the database

The app stores or updates the customer, call session, message log, AI summary, and either a lead or a pending booking request.

Owner receives action request

For a booking, the owner receives an SMS approval prompt and replies YES or NO. For non-booking leads, the owner receives a summary by the configured summary method.

Booking is confirmed or kept as a lead

YES confirms the job and sends the customer a confirmation SMS. NO moves the request to leads and no customer confirmation is sent.

Dashboard reflects the latest state

The owner dashboard reads jobs, customers, messages, call sessions, summaries, quotes, settings, and usage from Supabase.

Technical architecture

Frontend Surfaces

  • /owner: owner dashboard for jobs, leads, customers, calls, messages, settings, reminders, usage.
  • /admin: internal onboarding and account setup console.
  • /owner/login and /admin/login: Supabase Auth login screens.

Application Layer

  • Next.js App Router handles UI, server actions, and Twilio HTTP webhooks.
  • Realtime voice gateway handles Twilio Media Streams and OpenAI Realtime WebSocket sessions.
  • Shared libraries normalize phone numbers, route calls, geocode, translate summaries, and load dashboard data.

External Services

  • Supabase Auth, Postgres, RLS, and service-role writes.
  • Twilio Voice, Media Streams, Messaging, and status callbacks.
  • OpenAI Realtime for voice and Responses API for summary rewriting/translation.
  • Geocodio for address validation and service radius checks.
  • Resend for owner email summaries when configured.

Message Templates

The key outbound messages used in the current approval and summary flow.

Owner and customer communications

Owner Booking Approval SMS

Sent immediately when Ana creates a pending booking request.

{Ana} booking request for {Business Name}:
{Customer Name} ({Customer Phone})
{Service} - {Requested Date/Time}
Value: ${Estimated Value}
Address: {Service Address}
Reply YES to confirm and text the customer. Reply NO to keep it as a lead.

Customer Confirmation SMS

Sent only after the owner replies YES.

Hi {First Name}, this is {Ana} with {Business Name}. Your {Service} appointment is confirmed for {Requested Date/Time} at {Service Address}. Reply here if you need to update it.

Owner Summary SMS or Email

Sent through the configured owner summary method for appointment requests or leads.

Ana: New appointment request for {Service}

Caller: {Customer Phone}
Service: {Service}
Requested: {Requested Date/Time}
Name: {Customer Name}
Address: {Service Address}

Action: Review/approve before confirming.

Owner Contact Classification SMS

Used when Ana transfers an unknown caller and needs the owner to classify the number.

Was {Caller Phone} a personal contact? Reply YES to always send to me. Reply NO if this was a business caller.

Controls, Rules, and Failure Paths

Current guardrails that protect the business workflow and data quality.

Operational behavior

Scheduling Guardrails

  • Ana must collect a real full name before saving a booking or lead.
  • Ana must confirm the service address verbally before booking.
  • Ana checks business hours, closed dates, available slots, and service catalog.
  • Ana checks service radius when base address and radius are configured.
  • Ana creates pending jobs for owner approval, not final bookings.

Routing Guardrails

  • Known spam is rejected.
  • Known personal/vendor callers are transferred to the owner.
  • Unknown callers can be screened and classified.
  • If monthly AI call allowance is exceeded, the voice route stops accepting AI receptionist calls.
  • If SMS approval fails, the pending request is downgraded to a lead for manual follow-up.

Configuration Ownership

  • Admin owns onboarding, phone setup mode, routing numbers, Twilio Messaging Service SID, SMS/voice status, and billing plan.
  • Owner can adjust summary language, summary method, timezone, business hours, available services, service area, and booking records.
  • Supabase RLS limits dashboard reads/writes to authenticated members of the business.

Implementation Notes

  • Owner summary method can be SMS or email.
  • Booking approval is SMS-based because the owner must reply YES or NO.
  • SMS transition mode supports owner-forwarded texts using FWD +1CUSTOMERNUMBER plus the customer message.
  • Dashboard data freshness depends on Supabase reads and page refresh/revalidation behavior, not a separate event bus.
